Channels, cost, setup and the moments that need a person.

What is AI chatbot development?+

AI chatbot development is the process of building conversational bots powered by large language models and natural language understanding. The bots connect to your website, WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger and TikTok to automate customer support, lead capture and sales 24/7.

How do AI customer support chatbots reduce operating costs?+

AI support chatbots resolve up to 90% of routine inquiries such as order tracking, FAQs and policy questions instantly, without a human agent. That lowers ticket volume, cuts call center overhead and frees your team to handle complex or high-value cases.

Which messaging platforms can Syenxa Tech AI chatbots integrate with?+

Our chatbots work on websites built on any stack, WhatsApp Business API, Instagram DMs, Facebook Messenger, TikTok DMs and Shopify, and they sync with CRMs such as HubSpot, Salesforce and GoHighLevel.

Can a custom AI chatbot escalate complex issues to human agents?+

Yes. When a conversation needs human judgment or authorization, the chatbot hands the full transcript and customer context to your team through live chat, email or your CRM inbox, so nobody has to repeat themselves.

How much does a WhatsApp or Instagram AI chatbot cost?+

Syenxa Tech chatbots are a one-time setup fee between $150 and $350 USD with no monthly fee from us, plus a free 7-day live trial. Platform fees from Meta for WhatsApp Business messaging, where applicable, are billed by Meta directly.
