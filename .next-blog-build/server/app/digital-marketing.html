Before we get started

Search, campaigns and the details.

A few answers about the work, the channels and what progress can look like.

What does an AI marketing automation agency do?+

An AI marketing automation agency combines traditional digital marketing (SEO, content, paid ads) with automation such as AI chatbots, AI calling agents, CRM workflows and email or WhatsApp sequences, so leads are captured, qualified and followed up automatically.

What is included in Syenxa Tech's SEO services?+

Our SEO services cover a technical audit and fixes (Core Web Vitals, indexing, structured data), keyword research and content planning, on-page optimization, local SEO where relevant, and monthly reporting tied to leads rather than just rankings.

How long does SEO take to show results?+

Technical fixes can improve crawling and indexing within weeks. Meaningful ranking and traffic gains for competitive commercial keywords usually take 3 to 6 months of consistent content and link building.

Do you run paid ads as well as SEO?+

Yes. We run Google Search, Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and TikTok campaigns, and we connect every campaign to a chatbot or calling agent so paid traffic is followed up in seconds instead of hours.
