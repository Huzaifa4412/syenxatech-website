Built into every website

SEO, AEO and GEO: three ways to be found, one build.

SEO gets you ranked in Google. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) gets your page quoted as the answer in featured snippets and AI Overviews. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) gets you cited and recommended by ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Copilot. We build all three into the structure of the site rather than bolting them on later.

SEO

Search Engine Optimization

Where you show up

Google and Bing results

Server-rendered pages, keyword-mapped structure, internal linking, schema markup, Core Web Vitals and local SEO signals so each service page can rank in its city and category.

Ranks for the searches your customers type.

AEO

Answer Engine Optimization

Where you show up

Featured snippets, AI Overviews, voice search

Answer-first paragraphs, FAQ blocks, comparison tables and FAQPage schema so search engines can lift a direct answer from your page and name your business as the source.

Becomes the answer, not just a link.

GEO

Generative Engine Optimization

Where you show up

ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot

AI crawlers allowed, consistent entity data (who, where, what it costs), cited statistics, llms.txt and a machine-readable pricing file so AI assistants can read and cite you with confidence.

Performance

Why we build on Next.js

Speed and crawlability are ranking factors and conversion factors. Google found that 53% of mobile visits are abandoned when a page takes longer than three seconds to load (Google / SOASTA mobile speed benchmarks). Server rendering, image optimization and code splitting keep our sites well under that line, and put your content in the HTML on the first request so search engines and AI crawlers read it immediately.

Target Lighthouse scores, every build

Content Google can read immediately

Titles, headings, copy and structured data are rendered on the server, so nothing waits on JavaScript to be indexed.

Images that do not slow the page

Automatic AVIF and WebP conversion, responsive sizes and lazy loading for everything below the fold.

No plugin upkeep or security patches

A small modern codebase on a global edge network. Fewer moving parts, fewer outages, lower maintenance.

Built to extend

Add a booking system, chatbot, calling agent, blog or customer portal later without rebuilding.

Process

From first call to indexed in Google

Six steps, each with a clear deliverable. Standard business websites go live in 5 to 7 working days.

  1. 01

    Discovery call

    A free 30-minute call to understand your business, competitors and the searches you need to win. You get a fixed quote and delivery date.

  2. 02

    Keyword and answer map

    We map every page to a primary search intent and list the questions AI assistants get asked about your service, so the structure is decided before design.

  3. 03

    Design and content

    Custom mobile-first layouts, brand typography and imagery, and copy written answer-first so it reads well to people and extracts cleanly for AI.

  4. 04

    Build on Next.js

    Server-rendered code with metadata, sitemap, JSON-LD schema, llms.txt and image optimization built in. You review on a live staging link.

  5. 05

    Launch and index

    Domain, hosting and analytics connected, redirects handled, Search Console and Bing Webmaster submitted on day one.

  6. 06

    Support

    Post-launch fixes included. Optional care plans cover new pages, content refreshes and ongoing SEO.
