## Syenxa Tech Launches Enterprise-Grade AI Calling Agents & Automation Platform to Transform B2B Sales and Customer Support

**SAN FRANCISCO, CA & TORONTO, ON — July 28, 2026** — Syenxa Tech, a leading AI automation agency specializing in custom conversational artificial intelligence and high-performance software engineering, today announced the launch of its autonomous [AI Calling Agents](https://www.syenxatech.com/ai-calling-agents) platform.

Designed to address critical speed-to-lead delays and rising call center overhead, Syenxa Tech’s new voice AI agents provide businesses with 24/7 global phone coverage, instantaneous lead qualification, and real-time CRM calendar booking.

### Revolutionizing Sales Outreach with Sub-Second Voice AI

Unlike rigid interactive voice response (IVR) systems of the past, Syenxa Tech’s [AI Voice Agents](https://www.syenxatech.com/ai-calling-agents) engage in fluid, human-grade conversations with latency under 800 milliseconds. Built on proprietary natural language pipelines, the agents handle complex sales inquiries, answer questions, screen prospects, and automatically log booking data into platforms such as Salesforce, HubSpot, and Google Calendar.

"Speed to lead determines conversion rates in today’s fast-paced digital economy," said a spokesperson for [Syenxa Tech](https://www.syenxatech.com/). "Our AI calling agents ensure that no incoming lead goes to voicemail and no outbound sales opportunity is delayed."
