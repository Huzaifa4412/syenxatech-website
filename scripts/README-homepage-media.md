## Generation briefs

**Calling service:** Premium 16:10 editorial studio photograph of an orange
analog telephone handset resting on ivory travertine. Warm plaster studio,
soft natural daylight, realistic tactile materials and quiet shadows. Brand
palette: warm ivory `#faf9f7`, orange and charcoal. No people, text, logos,
interface, neon or technology grids.

**Chatbot service:** Premium 16:10 still life of two physical conversation
sculptures, orange translucent acrylic and white ceramic, leaning together on
an ivory stone pedestal. Warm daylight, precise shadows and tactile materials.
No text, logos, interface, message dots, neon or decorative technology grids.

**Industry reception:** Photorealistic 4:3 image of a welcoming modern local
business reception. Warm oak counter, orange analog telephone, appointment
notebook and a plant in the foreground. Two people greet each other softly out
of focus in the background. Natural daylight and ivory finishes. No readable
text, company branding, identifiable location or medical procedures.

The hero's acoustic-wave artwork is reused in the company and contact sections.
Its original generation prompt is documented in `README-call-demo.md`.
