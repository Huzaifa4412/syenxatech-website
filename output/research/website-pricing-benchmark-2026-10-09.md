## Decision

Retain Syenxa Tech's existing $200 entry price. Offer a clearly scoped Business estimate for up to 5 standard pages, a Grow estimate of $400 for 8 pages and a basic content editor, and a custom quote route for stores, portals and bespoke projects. This positions the entry build below these verified providers' entry build fees; it does not establish like-for-like savings or profitability.

Use an editable calculator rather than competitor price claims on the sales page. Implemented planning rates: $35 per extra standard page, $95 content editor, $95 booking-provider connection, $150 starting website chatbot setup, $125 lead-to-CRM connection. These rates are business positioning choices, not figures derived from competitor add-on schedules. Scope review remains necessary for custom integrations, migrations, languages and store requirements. The user authorized market research and customer-attracting pricing rather than supplying exact package rates.

Customer-facing emphasis: custom design, responsive layouts, technical SEO, enquiry capture, launch setup, ownership and a fixed quote before work begins. State that hosting, domain and third-party provider fees are separate. Do not invent a limited-time discount, popularity statistic, conversion guarantee or revenue promise.
